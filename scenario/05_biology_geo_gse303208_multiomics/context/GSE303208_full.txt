!Series_summary = We performed scRNA sequencing to understand the cellular heterogeneity, immune response, intracellular microbes associated with immune cells and  their role in COVID-19 pathophysiology. We combine this with Ab-Seq, oligo-attached antibody-based quantification of cell surface proteins to better understand the cell type and state of the cells. Samples were multiplexed using BD single-cell multiplexing kit (Human) in order to measure these data simultaneously
!Series_overall_design = PBMCs were isolated from Healthy (n = 9), COVID-19 (n = 24), recovered (n = 16) and longitudinal recovered (n = 8) individuals. PBMCs were isolated using Histopaque®-1077 (Sigma-Aldrich) density gradient centrifugation. Samples were multiplexed using BD single cell multiplexing kit (Human), followed by tagging with a pool of 50 oligo-attached antibody for surface markers (Ab-Seq). Around 25000 cells were loaded per cartridge, followed by cell lysis, cDNA synthesis and final library preparation using BD WTA amplification kit as per manufacturer's recommendation. A poly-A transcripts library (WTA), Ab-Seq library and library from oligos of Sampe multiplexing kit (SMK) were constructed from each batch, and were sequenced on NovaSeq 6000 platform.
