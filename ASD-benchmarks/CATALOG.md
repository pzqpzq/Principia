[Overview](README.md) · [Download guide](DOWNLOAD.md) · [CSV](CATALOG.csv) · [JSON](CATALOG.json) · [Protocol](EVALUATION_PROTOCOL.md)

The **Data** link opens the retained files; **Card** gives the scientific scope, validation units and reader limits; the scenario title links to its authoritative source. Sizes use decimal MB and describe the retained selection. “Expanded” counts unpacked source archives without permanently duplicating them. Observed includes source-calibrated and author-derived measurements, as detailed in each card. Mixed cases contain both measured and modeled products.

Original cases 01–20 were used during Principia development. Case 05 is a licensed replacement, case 19 uses a revised selection, and all acquisition substitutions are disclosed in [SUBSTITUTIONS.json](SUBSTITUTIONS.json). No case is guaranteed to contain an unknown law.
