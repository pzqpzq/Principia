# P100-048: SURFRAD radiation and weather across two sites and seasons

How do weather and site conditions affect radiation partitioning over complete days?

## Source preprocessing

NOAA calibrated station observations, commonly one-minute records, with source flags. The selection uses whole calendar months at two fixed sites and does not filter weather or quality.

## Prior analyses and limitations

Radiation partitioning has extensive established theory. Geometric radiation identities are not new findings. Two sites and two months leave seasonal/regional confounding and cannot establish climate trends. January/July 2026 Desert Rock/Penn State SURFRAD observations, calibrated and flagged. Shared radiation terms can create identities.
