## Source preprocessing

PPG was extracted from smartphone video; signals were manually synchronized and segmented by source authors. PPG 30 Hz, ECG 1,000 Hz, ACC 100 Hz per documentation. The package is not raw video.

## Prior analyses and limitations

The database was designed for PPG quality and heart-rate evaluation and has existing published analyses. Source quality flags mark some recordings unreliable; they are retained. Segments from a participant are strongly dependent; clinical deployment is outside acquisition acceptance. Read-only checks found 48 ECG and 48 PPG headers declaring a single frame with 10,000 or 300 channels, respectively; the other 3,840 recordings use conventional multiframe layouts. Header/data byte lengths and all 27,125 publisher-listed member hashes match. Preserve this source orientation difference. BUT smartphone PPG/WFDB and annotations; processed/reference products have distinct roles.
