# P100-040: New York City green-taxi trips in 2025

How do trip characteristics relate to travel time and demand across zones and periods?

## Source

https://www.nyc.gov/site/tlc/about/tlc-trip-record-data.page

## Source version

2025 complete green-taxi calendar year

## Origin

measured_or_observed

## Measurement dates

All 12 calendar months of 2025, retained as the agency's current frozen snapshot.

## Variables and units

Variables and units are preserved in native column headers, instrument metadata and source dictionaries; see evidence anchors below.

## Independent units

Zone pairs, dates and complete temporal blocks.

## Validation design

Zone pairs, dates and complete temporal blocks. Verify identifiers before allocating groups; the partition is not executed or frozen by this acquisition.

## Source preprocessing

TLC provider-submitted administrative records; data may contain reporting errors and agency/provider processing. Monthly files are retained whole, including flagged, implausible or incomplete records.

## Prior analyses and limitations

TLC trip data have extensive prior literature. Fare totals and trip durations have definitional components; memorizing those does not demonstrate scientific discovery. Anonymous trips do not identify individual travelers, and provider/zone/time effects matter. Twelve 2025 NYC green-taxi Parquets and zone context; operator-reported records contain known anomalies. Trips lack full road/traffic exposure.

## Missingness

Native blanks, sentinels, absent records and source quality flags are preserved. No imputation performed by the curator. Exact missingness semantics come from source dictionaries/headers; no universal missing-value rule is imposed.

## Calibration

Use the retained source calibration files/standards and instrument metadata where provided. Absence of an explicit calibration record is not replaced by an assumed calibration.
