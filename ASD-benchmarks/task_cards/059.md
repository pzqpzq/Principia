## Source preprocessing

Source workbooks, weather files, figures and processing code. Specific energy/avoided emissions and source plots are derived; supplied code is preserved, not run. The archive weather fields have an unresolved upstream provider and may be modeled/reanalysis products; they are not counted as measured-only evidence.

## Prior analyses and limitations

Hold out whole plants or time blocks. Verify weather-provider attribution and join timestamps before modeling. Derived emissions cannot be independent evidence for energy laws. Primary eligibility rests on the measured PV production and plant metadata; use weather only with its unresolved origin explicitly bounded. 

## Missingness

Native blanks, sentinels, absent records and source quality flags are preserved. No imputation performed by the curator. Exact missingness semantics come from source dictionaries/headers; no universal missing-value rule is imposed.

## Calibration

Use the retained source calibration files/standards and instrument metadata where provided. Absence of an explicit calibration record is not replaced by an assumed calibration.
