# P100-035: Ungulate vocalizations and production context

How do acoustic relationships transfer across animals and recording contexts?

Source: https://zenodo.org/records/14636641

Version: 14636641

Measurement coverage: Multiple original studies and acquisition periods, not one 2025 recording campaign. Deposit 2025.

Data origin: measured_or_observed

Source processing: Extracted/segmented animal contact calls rather than continuous microphone streams; source contextual labels and original filenames are retained. The source spelling WidlBoar is not corrected.

Interpretation limits: The source study is DOI 10.1016/j.isci.2025.111834 (PMC11847267). Valence is a context-derived label, not direct access to animal internal state. Animal, study and recording conditions may confound acoustic associations. 3,181 extracted ungulate WAV calls and metadata; call extraction is author preprocessing. Calls from one animal are dependent.
