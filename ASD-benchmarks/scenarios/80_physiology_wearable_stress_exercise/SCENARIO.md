# P100-080: Wearable device measurements during stress and exercise

How do physiological responses and sensor reliability vary across stress and exercise protocols?

Source: https://physionet.org/content/wearable-device-dataset/1.0.1/

Version: 1.0.1

Measurement coverage: Calendar dates are shifted; use relative/session timing. Dataset version released June 2025.

Data origin: measured_or_observed

Source processing: Native wearable exports plus annotations/notebook and known bad-trial descriptions. Different protocols have overlapping participant populations that require identifier reconciliation.

Interpretation limits: Participant-level grouping across protocols; then complete session/time blocks. Deidentified observations do not support clinical diagnostic claims.
