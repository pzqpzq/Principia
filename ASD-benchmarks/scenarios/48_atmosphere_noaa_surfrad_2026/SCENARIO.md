# P100-048: SURFRAD radiation and weather across two sites and seasons

How do weather and site conditions affect radiation partitioning over complete days?

Source: https://gml.noaa.gov/grad/surfrad/

Version: January and July 2026; Desert Rock NV and Penn State PA

Measurement coverage: All January and July 2026 days at Desert Rock NV and Penn State PA; daily files carry source year/day/time fields.

Data origin: measured_or_observed

Source processing: NOAA calibrated station observations, commonly one-minute records, with source flags. The selection uses whole calendar months at two fixed sites and does not filter weather or quality.

Interpretation limits: Radiation partitioning has extensive established theory. Geometric radiation identities are not new findings. Two sites and two months leave seasonal/regional confounding and cannot establish climate trends. January/July 2026 Desert Rock/Penn State SURFRAD observations, calibrated and flagged. Shared radiation terms can create identities.

Terms: U.S. government public-domain observations; NOAA acknowledgment
