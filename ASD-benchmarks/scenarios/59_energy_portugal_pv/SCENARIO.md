# P100-059: Photovoltaic Power Production Dataset

How do plant characteristics and weather relate to photovoltaic production across sites and seasons?

Source: https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/dbh93b6vp8/3

Version: 3

Measurement coverage: Original plant/weather files predate the February 2025 v3 release; exact coverage is in workbook/archive time columns.

Data origin: mixed_measured_and_computational

Source processing: Source workbooks, weather files, figures and processing code. Specific energy/avoided emissions and source plots are derived; supplied code is preserved, not run. The archive weather fields have an unresolved upstream provider and may be modeled/reanalysis products; they are not counted as measured-only evidence.

Interpretation limits: Hold out whole plants or time blocks. Verify weather-provider attribution and join timestamps before modeling. Derived emissions cannot be independent evidence for energy laws. Primary eligibility rests on the measured PV production and plant metadata; use weather only with its unresolved origin explicitly bounded.
