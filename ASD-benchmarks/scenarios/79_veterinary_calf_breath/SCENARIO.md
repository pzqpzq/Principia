# P100-079: Antibiotics Confound Breath-Based Respiratory Disease Detection in Calves - Dataset

How do treatment timing and health state affect calibrated breath-chemical responses within and across calves?

Source: https://zenodo.org/records/17940806

Version: 17940806

Measurement coverage: Date/time columns supplied in workbook; deposit December 2025, later modified April 2026.

Data origin: measured_or_observed

Source processing: PTR-QiTOF-derived emission rates, not raw mass spectra. Units differ: most VOCs micrograms/animal/hour, formaldehyde milligrams/animal/hour, CO2 grams/animal/hour.

Interpretation limits: Keep animals across repeated measurements and phases together where IDs match. Antibiotic treatment is a known confounder in the source paper.
