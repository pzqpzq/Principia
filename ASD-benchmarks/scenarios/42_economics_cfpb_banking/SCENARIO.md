# P100-042: National Age-Friendly Banking Survey

How do service access and digital use relate across banking-survey groups?

Source: https://www.consumerfinance.gov/data-research/national-age-friendly-banking-survey-data/

Version: March 2026 public-use release; collected January-February 2024

Measurement coverage: Survey fieldwork January 9–February 9, 2024; public-use files released March 2026.

Data origin: measured_or_observed

Source processing: Deidentified, weighted and recoded public-use survey products. The two CSVs describe the same respondents, not two independent samples. Financial values may be categories or ranges.

Interpretation limits: The technical report contains published summary statistics. Cross-sectional associations cannot establish whether digital banking causes service access changes.
