# P100-096: BikeZ-ETH - A Mass-Cycling Trajectory Dataset from a Controlled Experiment

How do lane width and crowding relate to cyclist trajectories across controlled sequences?

Source: https://zenodo.org/records/18098714

Version: 18098714

Measurement coverage: September 6, 2024; deposited December 2025.

Data origin: measured_or_observed

Source processing: Twenty-one TXT sequences extracted by computer vision/Kalman processing; large original video collection omitted.

Interpretation limits: Keep complete sequences and cyclist IDs together where available. Twenty-eight cyclists appear across sequences; tracker occlusions/identity ambiguity constrain individual-level inference.
