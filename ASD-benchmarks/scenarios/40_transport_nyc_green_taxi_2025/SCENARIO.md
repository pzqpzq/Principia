# P100-040: New York City green-taxi trips in 2025

How do trip characteristics relate to travel time and demand across zones and periods?

Source: https://www.nyc.gov/site/tlc/about/tlc-trip-record-data.page

Version: 2025 complete green-taxi calendar year

Measurement coverage: All 12 calendar months of 2025, retained as the agency's current frozen snapshot.

Data origin: measured_or_observed

Source processing: TLC provider-submitted administrative records; data may contain reporting errors and agency/provider processing. Monthly files are retained whole, including flagged, implausible or incomplete records.

Interpretation limits: TLC trip data have extensive prior literature. Fare totals and trip durations have definitional components; memorizing those does not demonstrate scientific discovery. Anonymous trips do not identify individual travelers, and provider/zone/time effects matter. Twelve 2025 NYC green-taxi Parquets and zone context; operator-reported records contain known anomalies. Trips lack full road/traffic exposure.

Terms: NYC Open Data terms; attribution retained
