# P100-039: Adult skills, workplace use and earnings in USA and Japan

How do measured skills and workplace use relate to earnings across population groups?

Source: https://www.oecd.org/en/data/datasets/piaac-2nd-cycle-database.html

Version: Cycle 2 public-use files; December 2024 release

Measurement coverage: Cycle 2 collection September 2022–August 2023; USA/Japan CSV release December 2024. Current retrieval freezes exact bytes; other countries received later revisions.

Data origin: measured_or_observed

Source processing: Anonymous public-use survey records: source weighting, proficiency estimation, derivation, coarsening and suppression have already occurred. These are not raw interview recordings.

Interpretation limits: Existing OECD reports already study skills and earnings. Two countries are not global coverage; country-specific suppression and earnings coding affect comparability.
