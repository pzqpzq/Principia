1. Title of Dataset: Density_and_refractive_index_profiles

Data were collected in the Laboratory of Hydrodynamic Micromodels, Institute of Geophysica, Polish Academy of Sciences
Dataset contains density profiles and refractive index (RI) profiles of water columns measured in each experimental set.
