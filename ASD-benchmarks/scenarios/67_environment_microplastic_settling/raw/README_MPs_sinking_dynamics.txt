1. Title of Dataset: MPs_sinking_dynamics
Data were collected in Laboratory of Hydrodynamic Micromodels, Institute of Geophysics, Polish Academy of Sciences

2. Folder List: 

MPs_averaged_position
MPs_averaged_velocity
MPs_position
MPs_velocity

3. Additional related data collected that was not included in the current data package: 
- Data on rheological properties of solutions stored in the 'Rheological_data' folder
- Data on profiles of density and refractive index stored in 'Density_and_refractive_index_profiles' folder
