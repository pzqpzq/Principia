# P100-067: Dataset on settling dynamics of microplastic particles sinking through exopolymer-induced rheological stratification

How do particle shape and fluid stratification affect settling trajectories and transition behavior?

Source: https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/ccm9k8pjft/1

Version: 1

Measurement coverage: Measurement files were created in 2024; deposit release March 2025.

Data origin: measured_or_observed

Source processing: Numerical PTV trajectories, density/refractive-index profiles and rheology. Complete numerical archives retained; sample-video archive omitted by modality rule.

Interpretation limits: Group particle and entire experiment; nine configurations span Newtonian and non-Newtonian layers. PTV positions are extracted measurements, not raw video pixels.
