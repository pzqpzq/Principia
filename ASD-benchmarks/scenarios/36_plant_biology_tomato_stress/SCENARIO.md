# P100-036: Dwarf tomato stress imaging and phenotypic measurements

How do imposed stress and acquisition conditions relate to plant-image measurements?

Version: 20638584

Measurement coverage: Metadata.xlsx spans 2023-11-27 to 2024-06-17 08:47:49; 347 records from 81 plant IDs. Released 2026-06-11.

Data origin: measured_or_observed

Source processing: NPQ pixel products and an integrated source-derived phenotypic table; RGB images and Micro-Tom NPQ images are omitted under the size limit. Supplied morphology/indices are already extracted, not instrument voltages.

Interpretation limits: The data accompany Image-based biomarkers effectively predict salt and drought stress in dwarf tomatoes. Metadata and main workbook use potentially inconsistent DAS/DAG definitions/order; retain both and reconcile from protocol before analysis. One cultivar has included images; metadata for the other is not missing imagery to invent. Tomato stress imaging reserve package; image-derived features require independent extraction and validation, not pixel-level random splits.
