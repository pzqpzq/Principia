# Rights and attribution register

No single license is asserted over the source corpus. `LICENSE_REGISTER.json` records each asset, hash, source license/terms, citation and rights-evidence URL. `CITATIONS.json` provides the corresponding researcher, title and version attribution.

Deposited data use the license recorded by the authoritative repository. Separate software notices remain controlling, including MIT, Apache 2.0 and source-specific licenses. Government/provider terms, CC BY attribution, CC BY-SA share-alike and ODC database attribution must be preserved where applicable. No endorsement by a source institution is implied.

Zenodo repository metadata is CC0 with an exception for email addresses ([policy](https://about.zenodo.org/policies/)); Figshare metadata is CC0 ([policy](https://info.figshare.com/user-guide/figshare-policies/)). Metadata snapshots with contacts or without an established reuse basis are retained locally and excluded from the release. `PUBLIC_CONTEXT_EXCLUSIONS.json` records these decisions. A minimal factual source profile supplies identifiers, inventory and attribution in their place. This metadata curation does not alter scientific payloads.

Curator-written documentation is licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/); curator tooling is under the [MIT license](tools/LICENSE). Attribute the collection to **Principia contributors**, identify the version, link to this repository, and indicate changes. These terms took effect upon owner acceptance on 2026-09-15. They do not relicense third-party assets. The repository's application licenses do not apply to third-party benchmark data. Frozen acquisition profiles may refer to proposed curator terms; this notice records their accepted publication status.

An open repository's deposit license is evidence of the contributor's stated permission, not an independent audit of every upstream right or scientific assertion. More specific embedded notices must be retained. Case 59's weather provider remains unidentified; this auxiliary product is explicitly excluded from measured-only evidence and its reuse claim is limited to the depositor's stated CC BY 4.0 terms. Primary research eligibility rests on the measured PV records. Case 05's former GEO deposit is not included because an adequate dataset redistribution grant was not established.
