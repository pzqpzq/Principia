Data Overview
-------------

Key to interpreting the data:
-file "Figure 1-Conversion rate.xlsx": Conversion rate as a function of temperature at different heating rates.  The heating rates are labeled in row 1.  Temperature columns are marked.  Raw conversion rates and fitted conversion rates are also marked as column headers.

-file "Figure 2-Conversion.xlsx": Conversion as a function of temperature at different heating rates.  The heating rates are labeled in row 1.  Temperature columns are marked.  Raw conversion and fitted conversion are also marked as column headers.

-file "Figure 3-Residue heat.xlsx": DSC heat flow curves of a) isothermal measurement at 120 °C for 8 hours and b) heating scan at 10 °C/min.  The sub-figure titles are labeled in row 1.  Time, temperature, and heat flow are marked as column headers.  Columns A and B correspond to data for Fig. 3a). Columns D and E correspond to data for Fig. 3b).

-file "Figure 4-FTIR spectra.xlsx": Time-resolved FT-IR spectra. Wavenumber data (x-axis) are listed in column A.  Columns B through H correspond to y-axis absorbance data at different times, as marked in row 1.  

-file "Figure 5-Normalized IR absorbance.xlsx": Time profiles of the normalized IR absorbances.  Time data (x-axis) are listed in column A.  Columns B through E correspond to y-axis normalized absorbance (unitless) for different reactants and the product, as labeled in row 1.

-file "Figure 6-Raman spectra.xlsx": Time-resolved Raman spectra.  Raman shift (x-axis) is listed in column A.  Columns B through F correspond to y-axis intensity with arbitrary units at different times, as labeled in row 1.

-file "Figure 7-Rheo-Raman data.xlsx": Rheo-Raman measurements data.  Time columns are marked.  Columns B, E, and F correspond to viscosity and modulus data plotted in the upper figure.  Columns J and K correspond to normalized peak heights at 788 and 1260 cm-1, plotted in the bottom figure.  Columns L and N are their respective standard errors.
