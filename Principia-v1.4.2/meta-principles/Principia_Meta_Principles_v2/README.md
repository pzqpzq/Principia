## Important interpretation

These are broad roots, not authority tokens. A Nobel Prize, Fields Medal, Turing Award, industrial roadmap, or high citation count is evidence of influence—not proof that every downstream application is correct. The corpus therefore keeps theorem, mechanism, empirical observation, heuristic, and hypothesis separate and makes boundary conditions mandatory.
