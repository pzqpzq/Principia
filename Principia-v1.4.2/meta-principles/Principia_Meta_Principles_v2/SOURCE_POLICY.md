# Source Policy

The corpus prioritizes original papers, books, official award pages, authoritative reviews, standards, and institutional sources. It stores original editorial summaries and bibliographic links only; it does not bundle paper PDFs, abstracts, figures, or long quotations.

Recent and award-related records were cross-checked against official Nobel Prize, ACM, IMU, publisher, or primary-paper pages where available. URL presence is not a scientific endorsement. Every record remains `curated_draft` pending domain-expert review.
