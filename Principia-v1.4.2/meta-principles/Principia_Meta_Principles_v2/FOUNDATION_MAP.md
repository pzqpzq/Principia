# Foundation Map

Use the corpus as a layered root system rather than a flat authority list:

```text
paper-level Principle
    ├── formal or mechanistic root
    ├── statistical / causal / measurement root
    ├── scale / resource / transport boundary
    └── industry or socio-technical operating constraint
```

## Four foundation classes

1. **Formal limits and theorems** — prove a consequence under explicit axioms or models.
2. **Mechanistic laws** — explain how a system changes under stated conditions.
3. **Empirical and industry laws** — summarize durable measured regularities; they can age or break.
4. **Governance and methodological principles** — control how evidence, metrics, experiments, and organizations should be interpreted.

## Non-negotiable rule

> Prestige, award recognition, citation count, and industrial adoption never upgrade an empirical law into a theorem or remove its boundary conditions.
