## Editorial policy

- Broad claims are written conservatively and paired with explicit boundary conditions.
- Formal results are distinguished from empirical laws, hypotheses, and heuristics.
- Contested principles are marked `contested` or `context-dependent`.
- The corpus does not assign fabricated numeric validity or quality scores.
- Related works include originals when practical and authoritative refinements or reviews when historical originals are difficult to access.
- Relation seeds support retrieval and reasoning; they do not propagate truth or maturity automatically.

## Limitations

This is a broad foundation seed, not proof that every future Principle has one correct parent. Coverage is strongest for mainstream theoretical and methodological roots; specialized subfields will require additional topic packs. Evidence URLs were syntactically validated and selected from DOI resolvers, publishers, scholarly repositories, standards bodies, or official archives. Maintainers should still open every source and obtain domain-expert review before promotion into a reviewed Global package.
