# Source and Content Policy

This corpus stores original editorial summaries of Meta-Principles plus bibliographic titles and public URLs. It does **not** store paper PDFs, copied full text, figures, tables, or long quotations.

## Evidence roles

- `foundation`: an original or canonical formulation;
- `proof` / `formalization`: a proof-bearing or formal treatment;
- `refinement` / `extension`: a later work that narrows, generalizes, or qualifies the Principle;
- `review` / `synthesis`: an authoritative consolidation;
- `boundary` / `critique`: a work useful for understanding failure conditions or controversy.

A linked title is not itself validation. Curators should open the source, verify bibliographic identity, and confirm that the stored interpretation stays within the source's scope before publication as a reviewed Global Capsule.

## Compactness

The corpus is intentionally paper-free. Human-readable Markdown, JSONL records, deduplicated work identities, and relation seeds are sufficient for search and graph construction while keeping the foundation layer small. Full documents remain at their original publishers or repositories.

## Copyright and attribution

The summaries and annotations are newly written for this corpus. Source titles, authorship history, and URLs are retained for attribution and verification. Downstream publication should preserve links and comply with the license and access terms of each source.
