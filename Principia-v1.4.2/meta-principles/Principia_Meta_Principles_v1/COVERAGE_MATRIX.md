## Gap policy

When a new Principle cannot be linked without stretching a boundary, do not force a relation. Record the unmatched mechanism or theorem as a coverage gap, then add a reviewed Meta-Principle or a more specialized topic pack. Repeated `ungrounded` outcomes in one topic are evidence that the corpus needs expansion.
